## A Memorable Day Before the Final Examinations

Before our final examinations, our department organized an exhibition where students were given the opportunity to present their technical projects. For me, this exhibition became one of the most memorable experiences of the semester because it allowed us to demonstrate what we had learned in a practical way instead of only writing answers in examinations.

Our group presented a **Kidney Disease Prediction System**, a project that combined Machine Learning, database management, and web development. It was exciting to explain our work to visitors, teachers, and fellow students because the project represented many of the concepts we had been studying throughout the semester.

The exhibition reminded me that computer science is not only about theory. Real learning happens when knowledge is applied to solve practical problems.

## Choosing a Healthcare Problem

When selecting our project, we wanted to work on something that could have a real impact. After discussing different ideas, we decided to develop a system for predicting **Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)**.

Kidney disease is a serious medical condition, and early prediction can help patients receive timely medical attention. Although our project was developed for educational purposes, it demonstrated how Machine Learning can support healthcare by analyzing patient information and providing predictions.

Working on a healthcare-related project also made us realize how technology can contribute to solving real-world challenges.

## Building the Machine Learning Model

The heart of our project was the Machine Learning model.

Before training the model, we first studied the dataset carefully. We understood that machine learning depends on high-quality data, so preparing the dataset was just as important as selecting the algorithm itself.

The model was trained using patient information so that it could identify patterns associated with Chronic Kidney Disease. During this process, I revised many concepts from my first semester, including data preprocessing, model training, testing, evaluation, and prediction.

This project helped me understand that Machine Learning is much more than writing code. It requires patience, experimentation, and continuous improvement.

## Developing the User Interface Through an API

Another interesting part of our project was connecting the Machine Learning model with a user-friendly interface.

Instead of interacting directly with the model, we used an API to connect the backend prediction system with the frontend application.

This allowed users to enter their medical information through a simple interface. After submitting the required details, the application sent the data to the trained Machine Learning model through the API. The model processed the information and returned a prediction indicating whether the patient was likely to have Chronic Kidney Disease.

This practical implementation helped me understand how Machine Learning models become real applications that ordinary users can interact with.

## The Importance of Database Knowledge

While working on the project, I also realized how valuable my Database Systems knowledge had become.

Patient information needs to be stored, managed, and organized properly before it can be used for analysis. Concepts such as database tables, relationships, primary keys, and structured data became directly relevant to our project.

The connection between Database Systems and Machine Learning became much clearer during this experience. A reliable Machine Learning model depends on well-organized data, and well-organized data begins with good database design.

This practical project strengthened my appreciation for both subjects.

## Learning Through Teamwork

The exhibition project also taught me the importance of teamwork.

Every member of our group contributed according to their responsibilities. We discussed ideas together, solved technical problems, tested the system, and improved different parts of the project before presenting it.

Whenever we faced challenges, we worked together to find solutions instead of giving up. This collaborative environment helped us complete the project successfully and prepared us for presenting it confidently.

I realized that software development is rarely an individual effort. Communication and cooperation are just as important as technical knowledge.

## Guidance from Dr. Bilal Ahmad

Throughout the development of our project, the guidance of Dr. Bilal Ahmad played an important role in shaping our understanding of practical learning.

His emphasis on Machine Learning, model training, and solving real-world problems encouraged us to think beyond classroom assignments. Instead of focusing only on theoretical concepts, he motivated students to understand how technology can be applied in meaningful ways.

His approach toward concept-based learning helped me connect Database Systems, Machine Learning, APIs, and software development into one complete project. This experience increased my confidence and motivated me to continue learning advanced technologies in the future.

## Presenting the Project

Presenting the project during the exhibition was a completely different experience from building it.

Visitors asked questions about how the Machine Learning model worked, how patient information was processed, and how predictions were generated. Explaining these concepts helped me realize how much I had learned during the semester.

Standing beside our project and answering questions gave me confidence in my technical communication skills. It also showed me the importance of understanding concepts deeply instead of memorizing them.

The exhibition transformed months of learning into a practical demonstration that others could see and appreciate.

## Lessons I Will Always Remember

Looking back, the department exhibition became much more than a project presentation. It represented the progress I had made throughout the semester.

The project combined Machine Learning, model training, APIs, database concepts, and practical software development into one meaningful application. It showed me that classroom learning becomes much more valuable when it is applied to solve real problems.

This experience also motivated me to continue improving my technical skills in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Database Systems, and full-stack development. I hope to build even more advanced healthcare applications in the future that can contribute to solving real-world challenges.

Participating in the exhibition was one of the proudest moments of my academic journey, and it strengthened my belief that continuous learning, teamwork, and practical implementation are the keys to becoming a successful software engineer.
