## Continuing the Journey After Mid Exams

After completing my mid examinations, I expected that the remaining semester would become easier. However, I soon realized that learning does not stop after exams. In fact, the period after the mids became even more important because it was the time to strengthen concepts instead of only preparing for tests. Although many of our database labs continued in online mode, I was surprised by how much practical learning was still possible.

Initially, I thought online labs would not be as effective as physical labs. I believed that technical subjects require face-to-face interaction to understand properly. However, my experience gradually changed because the lab sessions were designed to focus on practical implementation instead of only theoretical discussion.

## Online Labs That Felt Practical

One thing I appreciated during the second half of the semester was that the online database labs were not treated as ordinary online lectures. Every lab session involved practical work. Instead of simply reading slides, we were encouraged to create databases, write SQL queries, and solve problems step by step.

This practical approach kept me engaged throughout the sessions. Even though we were attending online, I never felt that we were only listening to lectures. Every class required participation, observation, and implementation.

I realized that practical learning is possible even in an online environment when the instructor focuses on understanding rather than memorization.

## Dr. Bilal Ahmad's Effort During Online Labs

One of the most valuable parts of these lab sessions was the effort made by Dr. Bilal Ahmad. Conducting online practical classes is not easy because students have different internet connections, different systems, and different learning speeds. Despite these challenges, he continued explaining every concept patiently.

Whenever a new SQL command or database concept was introduced, it was first explained carefully and then demonstrated through implementation. Instead of expecting students to memorize syntax, he showed how each command worked in practice and why it was needed.

This teaching style made the labs much more interesting because every concept was connected with a real task rather than remaining only a theoretical definition.

## Learning by Doing Instead of Memorizing

One lesson I learned from these labs was that technical education becomes meaningful only when students practice independently. Watching someone write SQL queries is helpful, but writing them yourself is a completely different experience.

During the lab sessions, we practiced creating tables, defining primary keys, inserting records, updating information, deleting unnecessary data, and retrieving records using different SQL queries. Every activity helped me become more comfortable with database operations.

Whenever I made mistakes in my queries, I learned something new while correcting them. Those small errors became valuable learning opportunities because they helped me understand the logic behind SQL syntax.

## Practical Implementation Improved My Confidence

Before attending these practical lab sessions, I often felt uncertain while writing SQL statements. I understood the theory, but I was not always confident about implementing it.

As the labs continued, my confidence gradually improved. Repeating different SQL commands helped me remember their syntax naturally. Instead of trying to memorize commands, I started understanding their purpose.

Simple queries became easier, and even more complex database tasks looked manageable after enough practice. This improvement showed me that confidence comes from repeated implementation rather than repeated reading.

## Understanding the Importance of Database Design

Another important lesson from the labs was understanding proper database design. We learned that creating a database is not only about making tables. Every table should have a clear purpose, relationships should be properly defined, and unnecessary duplication should be avoided.

While implementing database concepts practically, topics like primary keys, foreign keys, relationships, and normalization became much easier to understand. These ideas had seemed theoretical during lectures, but after implementing them in the lab, I finally understood why they are so important.

This practical experience changed the way I viewed Database Systems as a subject.

## Connecting Database Skills with Future Technologies

As I continued learning through these practical sessions, I also started thinking about the future. Modern software applications depend on databases to manage information efficiently. Whether someone develops websites, mobile applications, business systems, or artificial intelligence solutions, databases play an essential role.

I also realized that machine learning projects require organized datasets before models can be trained successfully. Those datasets must first be stored, managed, and prepared using database concepts.

Because of this understanding, I became more motivated to improve both my SQL skills and my database knowledge. The labs helped me see that these skills are valuable far beyond university examinations.

## The Value of Consistent Practice

One habit that developed during these online labs was practicing regularly after every session. Instead of waiting until exams, I tried to repeat the queries demonstrated in class and experiment with my own examples.

Sometimes my queries produced errors, but instead of becoming frustrated, I treated every mistake as part of the learning process. Debugging incorrect SQL statements helped me understand the language more deeply.

Gradually, I realized that technical skills improve through continuous practice rather than occasional effort. This lesson is something I will continue applying throughout my academic journey.

## Looking Back on the Online Lab Experience

When I think about the online database labs after my mid exams, I no longer remember them as simply online classes. I remember them as sessions where practical learning became the main priority.

Despite the limitations of online education, the effort put into teaching and demonstrating concepts made every lab meaningful. Practical implementation helped transform theoretical knowledge into real understanding.

These sessions also taught me that successful learning depends on both the instructor's guidance and the student's willingness to practice consistently.

## A Lesson I Will Carry Forward

Looking back, I can confidently say that the online database labs after my mid examinations became one of the most valuable learning experiences of my semester. They strengthened my understanding of SQL, database design, normalization, and practical implementation.

Most importantly, they changed my attitude toward technical education. I now understand that true learning happens when concepts are practiced repeatedly until they become natural.

As I continue my journey in computer science, I hope to build more database projects, improve my SQL skills, and connect this knowledge with future fields such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and software development. The online labs proved that with proper guidance, sincere effort, and continuous practice, meaningful learning is possible regardless of whether the classroom is physical or online.
