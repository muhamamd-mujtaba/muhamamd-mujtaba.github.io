## A New Beginning After My Mid Exams

The mid examinations of my second semester became an important turning point in my academic life. Before the exams, I was mainly focused on completing the syllabus and preparing for different subjects within a short period of time. Like many students, I experienced stress because my preparation was not as organized as I wanted it to be. Although I worked sincerely during the mid week, I realized afterward that technical subjects require much more than last-minute preparation.

Instead of becoming disappointed, I decided to learn from my experience. I asked myself a simple question: "How can I improve my understanding instead of only improving my marks?" That question completely changed my approach toward learning. Rather than studying only for examinations, I started studying to build real knowledge that could help me throughout my university life and future career.

## Studying Database Systems More Deeply

After my mids, I started reading Database Systems much more seriously than before. During lectures I had understood the basic concepts, but after the exams I wanted to understand the logic behind every topic. I revised every chapter carefully, starting from database fundamentals and gradually moving toward advanced concepts.

As I continued studying, I realized that databases are the heart of almost every software application. Whether it is a banking system, hospital management system, university portal, or social media platform, all of them depend on well-designed databases.

I revised concepts such as entities, attributes, relationships, primary keys, foreign keys, SQL queries, normalization, and database design. Instead of memorizing definitions, I tried to understand why each concept exists and how it solves real-world problems.

This deeper study made Database Systems one of my favorite subjects during the semester.

## Revisiting My First Semester Machine Learning Learning

While studying databases, I also remembered many topics that I had learned during the first semester about Machine Learning. Instead of leaving those concepts behind, I decided to revise them together with my database studies.

During my revision, I reviewed the basic workflow of machine learning, including data collection, preprocessing, feature selection, model training, testing, evaluation, and prediction. Revising these concepts reminded me that machine learning is not only about algorithms. Every successful machine learning project begins with clean, organized, and reliable data.

That realization created a strong connection between my first semester learning and my second semester Database Systems course.

## Understanding the Importance of Model Training Again

One topic that attracted my attention once again was model training. During my first semester, I understood the basic idea of training a machine learning model. However, after revising it, I appreciated the concept much more deeply.

Model training is a continuous learning process. A model becomes better only after repeated training, testing, evaluation, and improvement. Poor-quality data leads to poor-quality predictions, while organized and accurate data helps models perform much better.

This idea helped me understand why databases are so important in artificial intelligence projects. Before a model can learn anything, the data must first be collected, stored, managed, and organized properly. Without an effective database, even the most advanced machine learning algorithm cannot produce reliable results.

## Discovering the Connection Between Databases and Artificial Intelligence

One of the most interesting discoveries I made after my mids was the relationship between Database Systems and Artificial Intelligence.

Previously, I had considered these subjects separately. Database Systems seemed to be about SQL and tables, while Machine Learning appeared to be about algorithms and predictions. However, as I studied more, I realized that these fields are closely connected.

Machine learning depends on high-quality datasets. Those datasets are usually stored and managed inside databases before they are processed. Data scientists spend a significant amount of time cleaning, organizing, and preparing data before training any model.

Because of this understanding, I began appreciating Database Systems even more. I realized that learning SQL and database design is not only useful for database administrators but also for future software engineers, AI engineers, and machine learning developers.

## Practical Learning Through Continuous Practice

Another important change after my mids was my study routine. Instead of only reading theory, I started practicing regularly.

I solved SQL queries repeatedly until writing SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE, and CREATE TABLE statements became comfortable. I also practiced aggregate functions, filtering records, sorting data, and understanding relationships between tables.

Similarly, while revising Machine Learning concepts, I reviewed how datasets are prepared before training a model. I understood that repeated practice is necessary in every technical field. Reading once is never enough. Real understanding develops only when concepts are applied multiple times.

This practical learning approach increased my confidence significantly.

## Looking Toward the Future

Today, I feel much more confident than I did before my mid exams. Those exams taught me that technical education is built through patience, consistency, and continuous revision. Every difficult topic becomes easier when studied with proper understanding and regular practice.

Database Systems has become one of my strongest interests, and revisiting Machine Learning concepts has strengthened my motivation to continue learning Artificial Intelligence in the future. I now understand that databases, machine learning, programming, and data analysis are all connected. Improving one skill automatically supports the others.

As I continue my academic journey, I hope to strengthen my SQL skills, improve my database design knowledge, learn advanced machine learning techniques, and work on practical AI projects. My mid exams were not simply an examination period—they became the beginning of a more focused, disciplined, and meaningful learning journey.
